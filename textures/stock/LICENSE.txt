The PNG files in this directory are from Kenney's "Retro Textures Fantasy" (1.0),
created and distributed by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), licensed Creative Commons Zero (CC0):
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

They stand in for the source game's own textures on imported maps; see substitutes.json
and game/g2g_stock_substitutes.gd. Nothing from the source game is in this directory.

The three JPEGs (cc0_concrete_smooth.jpg, cc0_metal_brushed.jpg, cc0_metal_plates.jpg) are
from ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com), by Lennart Demes, licensed CC0 1.0:
  cc0_concrete_smooth.jpg  Concrete034     https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Concrete034
  cc0_metal_brushed.jpg    Metal032        https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Metal032
  cc0_metal_plates.jpg     MetalPlates006  https://ambientcg.com/view?id=MetalPlates006
Each is the 1K colour map, scaled to 512 px. Kenney has no concrete or metal; the family
rule is Kenney first, then free CC0/MIT art from elsewhere.
